MMO Permutations

Catch and despawn paths for Massive Mass Outbreaks in Pokémon Legends: Arceus. Your ticks are saved in this browser.

C = catch it KO = defeat it cells sharing a colour must be defeated in one battle

Read a row left to right: each column is a spawn in the first wave. A run of same-coloured KO cells is one multi-battle — three blue means take all three down together. New paths stop once only four remain in the wave, which is where ghost steps take over.
